Evidence on Ireland’s health, from the Global Burden of Disease Study

Ireland Health Evidence brings the Global Burden of Disease (GBD) estimates for Ireland, published by the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME), together in one place: how health, disease and risk factors have changed over time, and which causes and risks weigh most on the population today.

It is developed by the School of Public Health at University College Cork. Every figure keeps its GBD release, uncertainty interval and source file, so it can be interpreted — and cited — with care.

What the application provides

Trends over time

Follow any GBD measure for Ireland by cause, risk factor, metric, age and sex, with its 95% uncertainty interval drawn around the estimate.

Leading causes and risks

See which causes of disease and which risk factors rank highest, for the year and population group you choose.

Citable downloads

Download the values behind every chart as CSV, or as PNG and PDF figures that carry the GBD release and the IHME citation.

Traceable to the source

Every dataset records its GBD release, the files it was built from, with checksums, and when it was last updated.

About the data

Estimates come from the IHME Global Burden of Disease Results Tool and are used under IHME’s Free-to-Use Data Terms. GBD is published in rounds, and the application records which release each figure comes from.

While the application is in development it runs on prototype data, and figures produced from it are marked as not for citation.
